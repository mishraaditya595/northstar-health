# ADR-0015 — Wear OS companion

**Status:** **Accepted** — approved by the project owner on 2026-11-27 (Adi approved the design). Stages 1 (the pure-Kotlin watch core), 2 (link and glance) and 3a (workout recording on the watch) are implemented in source, not released or device-verified; stage 3b and stages 4 and 5 are not started.
**Builds on:** [ADR-0012](0012-apple-watch-companion.md) (decisions 3, 5, 6 and 7 apply unchanged) and the planned ADR-0014 (the phone writes workouts to Health Connect; being designed in a separate thread and not in the repository yet). **Amends:** ADR-0012 decision 4 and [HEALTH_DATA.md](../HEALTH_DATA.md) when stage 3 lands. Design: [Wear OS companion](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-wear-os-companion-design.md).

## Decisions

1. **Native companion.** The Wear OS app is native Kotlin with Compose for Wear OS, built in `apps/wear_os/`. It is not Flutter.
2. **Same app ID and signing key as the phone.** The watch app's `applicationId` is `in.beyondrep.app` (the Kotlin namespace stays `in.beyondrep.wear`), because the Wear Data Layer only connects apps that share both. The watch build is signed with the phone's key.
3. **Same rules as the Apple Watch.** ADR-0012 decision 3 (completed watch workouts are backed up once, by the phone, after its durable import), decision 5 (HMAC account key; the watch has no Supabase session) and decision 6 (the device that starts a session owns it) apply to Wear OS. Decision 7 (shared formula library) is not reconsidered.
4. **Transport is the Wear Data Layer.** Application-context calls map to one `DataItem` per type at `/context/<type>`, queued transfers to `DataItem`s at `/queue/<direction>/<message_id>` deleted after handling, live messages to `MessageClient`, and reachability to `NodeClient` and the `beyondrep_watch_app` capability (design 5.2). A Data Layer delivery is not a phone ObjectBox or Supabase acknowledgement; the phone's `ack` message is.
5. **Heart rate and calories of a watch workout reach Health Connect through the phone** (design 4.3, including consent, retention and phone-owned workouts), not from the watch. This amends ADR-0012 decision 4 and HEALTH_DATA.md when stage 3 lands; nothing changes before then.
6. **One tested core.** `apps/wear_os/core` is a JVM-only Gradle module with no `android.*` import. It holds the contract codecs, replay, session machine, command applier, state and completed-workout builders, the phone inbox and the SQLite journal (over a small `SqlDatabase` interface). It runs the same golden fixtures and cases under `contracts/` as Swift and Dart. The journal has one schema version (Swift journal v8 without the `links` table, because Wear sends no `workout_link`).

## Stages

1. Watch core (this ADR's source: `:core` tests, app ID, placeholder UI only).
2. Link and glance: phone Data Layer bridge, watch Home and Train pages, phone watch status.
3. Workout recording with Health Services. Split: **3a** records and delivers the workout (heart rate and calories live on the watch, memory only); **3b** adds the `workout_health` message and the phone's Health Connect write of heart rate and calories (design 4.3).
4. Live link both ways.
5. The watch's own health (steps and heart rate through passive monitoring).

## Consequences

- Nothing is released or device-verified. Stage 1 adds no permission, no screen and no data flow: `AndroidSqlDatabase` exists but nothing calls it.
- `:app:assembleDebug` runs on GitHub CI only; the local container has no Android SDK.
- Not ported from Swift in stage 1: journal migrations from older Swift schema versions (no Wear database exists) and `workout_link`.
- Stage 2 adds the Wear Data Layer link and no permission: the phone app declares the capability `beyondrep_phone_app`, the watch `beyondrep_watch_app`, and both add `play-services-wearable`. Items are `/beyondrep/context/<type>` (latest wins; on sign-out the phone deletes every context item except a fresh `account_cleared` context item, which carries the clear and is removed by the next `updateContext`, so no stale binding or plans replay), `/beyondrep/queue/phone/<id>` and `/beyondrep/queue/watch/<id>` (deleted by the receiver after handling; a watch message stays until Dart has accepted it, so one sent while the phone app is not running arrives on the next start). The phone bridge hands watch messages to Dart only after Dart calls `startMessages`, which the watch sync controller does once it has subscribed (the wipe retrier subscribes at startup), so no queued item is delivered before its listeners exist. A Dart `error` or `notImplemented` reply drops the item from the bridge's memory only; it stays in the Data Layer and the next `startMessages` delivers it again, which is safe because Dart handles every message idempotently by `message_id`. A second `startMessages` while started does nothing. The phone keeps a message delivered before its screen-level listeners (live workout) subscribe only if the item is still queued, so those streams are not part of the start-up flush. The watch router applies a batch by type (binding, clear, plans, health) and then send time, as Swift's inbox does, because a health snapshot can be older than the binding. The watch deletes its own old acks beyond the newest 20 or older than 14 days; the phone deletes the rest as it accepts them. A deletion alone changes nothing on the watch: the phone clears it with a queued `account_cleared`, as an empty application context does on iOS. The watch reads no health itself, shows only the phone's `health_snapshot` (today and 7 days, cleared at local midnight and on sign-out, never logged) and has no Start button.
- Stage 2 distribution: the Play job builds the phone bundle with versionCode `N` and the watch bundle with `N + 1`, both with the upload key, so Play App Signing gives them one signing key. The watch bundle is drafted to the Wear OS form-factor track `wear:<track>`.
- **Stage 3a amendment (workout recording, implemented in source, not released or device-verified).** The watch starts a plan, today's program workout or a quick workout, logs sets with the bezel or crown, rests with one haptic per deadline, and sends the finished `completed_workout` (without `healthkit_workout_uuid`) until the phone's `ack`. The journal is the authority; a Health Services `ExerciseClient` session (strength training, weightlifting as fallback; heart rate and calories only where supported and allowed; no GPS, no auto-pause) exists only for sensors and to keep the process alive inside a foreground service of type `health` with an ongoing activity (text: workout name and set count). New manifest permissions: `BODY_SENSORS`, `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `VIBRATE`, `WAKE_LOCK`; they are requested once, before the first Start, and any denial still records without that sensor. `targetSdk` stays 35 (at 36 `BODY_SENSORS` is replaced by `READ_HEART_RATE`). **Heart rate and calories are memory only in 3a**: never in the journal, outbox, a message, a log line, a notification or a crash string; the summary shows an average heart rate only when one was measured. Another app's exercise blocks Start (nothing is journaled); a killed process restores from the journal and reattaches to or restarts the sensor session; sign-out mid-workout discards it. On Android 14 and later a `health` foreground service throws `SecurityException` without `BODY_SENSORS` or `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`, so with both denied the workout records without the service (the journal is still the record; the system may stop the process with the screen off and a relaunch restores it); the service is `START_STICKY` and restores the workout itself after a system restart. `WAKE_LOCK` is used for one thing: a partial wake lock (timeout: the rest left plus 5 s) held while a rest pulse is pending so the timer runs with the screen off; a killed process gets no pulse. Start, restore, finish, discard, account changes and actions run under one mutex, and a restore abandons active sessions of an account that is not bound or that the phone cleared. Only acks and undecodable items are pruned from the watch queue, never a queued `completed_workout`, and only the bound account's workouts are put on it. Stage 3b (the `workout_health` message, seven-day journal retention of samples, the phone's Health Connect write) will amend HEALTH_DATA.md further.
- The label is "Apple Watch" on iOS and "Watch" on Android (design 4.4); the cloud origin stays `beyondrep_watch`.

**Stage 3a open risks (from review, not fixed):** the UI and the ongoing-activity text read the mutable `WorkoutController` on the main thread while the journal thread writes it (benign copy-then-swap today; publish an immutable snapshot later); a workout put on the Data Layer while another account was bound stays there if the phone ignores it, since nothing deletes it; the heart rate and calories permission prompt shows once and a later grant is only picked up by the next workout.

- **Stage 3b amendment (workout heart rate and calories to Health Connect, implemented in source, not released or device-verified).** At Finish the watch also saves a `workout_health` message (contract `watch_link/v1`, `schema_version` stays 1): one average heart rate per minute (0 to 600 points, 25 to 250 bpm, inside the workout, strictly increasing) and optional total calories. It is stored in a journal row `session_health` (journal `user_version` 2) and resent with the same message id until the phone acks it; the phone sends it only after the `completed_workout` was delivered. **This supersedes "memory only" for the finished workout:** the values now live on the watch until the ack, on any ack for that message, on sign-out or binding drop, and at most 7 days after the workout ended (`pruneStale`); they are never in `completed_workout`, the outbox of workouts, logs, notifications or crash text, and live heart rate during the workout is still memory only. The phone (`WatchWorkoutHealthInbox`) checks the account and that the workout was imported from the watch and not deleted, checks that the message's start and end match the stored workout within 2 seconds, then writes the Phase 5 `HealthWorkoutWriter` entry with `totalEnergyBurned` and ONE native `HeartRateRecord` holding all samples (`HeartRateWriter.kt`, channel `beyondrep/health_heart_rate`, client record id `<uuid>:heart_rate`; the `health` plugin is not used for heart rate because it logs every bpm value and writes one record per call) and keeps only the local receipt; the Health Connect client record version of a write is `workout version * 1000 + attempt`, so a retry after a failed or partial write is a greater version and not ignored; values are held in memory during the write and never stored, uploaded or exported. Ack `stored` after a confirmed write, `duplicate` for a confirmed receipt at the same version, `rejected` with the new reason `not_written` (the workout exists and nothing will be written: export switch off, workout deleted, not Android, Health Connect not installed or unsupported), and no ack when the switch is on but heart rate or calories access is missing, for a transient failure, and inside the receipt backoff, so the watch keeps the series (up to the 7-day cap) and resends; it is written once the user allows access. The watch sends the message even when it has no heart rate and no calories, so the session itself reaches Health Connect; it also deletes the series when its `completed_workout` is rejected. An ack for another account (`wrong_account`) does not clear the watch's series: it is dropped by the 7-day prune (or on `account_cleared` or a binding drop). The export switch gates the write (ADR-0014); `WRITE_HEART_RATE` and `WRITE_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED` (and `READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED` for the history readback) are requested from the Health tab row "Allow heart rate and calories" when a Wear watch is paired. Deleting the workout removes the Health Connect workout, then best effort the calories (`<uuid>:calories`) and the one heart rate record (`<uuid>:heart_rate`); once the session is deleted a failure of the other two (heart rate access revoked, say) still counts as deleted, and a record left behind is only a value no session explains. Heart rate and calories are requested with read and write because the history detail reads them back (needs Phase 3 health connected and `READ_HEART_RATE`). The history detail of a Wear OS workout shows average and max heart rate and calories read fresh from Health Connect (own source only), never stored. iOS never sends or writes this message; the Swift codec only decodes it. The `decision 4` health-values rule of ADR-0012 is unchanged for the Apple Watch.
