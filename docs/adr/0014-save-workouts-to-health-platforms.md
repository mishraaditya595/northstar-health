# ADR-0014 — Save phone workouts to Apple Health and Health Connect (Phase 5)

**Status:** **Accepted** — approved by the human owner on 2026-11-27 (design and plan). Implemented in source, **not released or device-verified**.
**Builds on:** [ADR-0003](0003-data-cloud-health.md) (`WorkoutHealthExport` boundary), [ADR-0008](0008-daily-health-summary-reads.md), [ADR-0011](0011-read-workouts-from-health-platforms.md) and [ADR-0012](0012-apple-watch-companion.md). Does not change ADR-0005 backup scope. Design: [`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-phase-5-health-export-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-phase-5-health-export-design.md).

## Context

`docs/ROADMAP.md` Phase 5 asks for an opt-in write of one BeyondRep-owned workout summary, with export receipts and retry, never writing the Apple Watch `HKWorkout` twice and never writing imported workouts. Until now the app only read health data and `NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription` said it never wrote. Writing a record into the user's health store is a new permission and a policy change, so it needs a numbered ADR.

## Decision

1. **One switch per account, off by default.** "Save workouts to Apple Health" / "Save workouts to Health Connect" in the Health tab, independent of the health read connection and of cloud backup (three separate opt-ins). Turning it on opens a first-use sheet that says what is saved; only then is the platform prompt shown. A denied prompt keeps the switch on and shows "Allow access in Settings". Turning it off stops writes and deletes; entries already saved stay.
2. **What is written.** One strength-training workout per completed, non-deleted workout with origin `northstar_phone`, including all past ones on this phone and workouts restored from the cloud. iOS `TRADITIONAL_STRENGTH_TRAINING`, Android `STRENGTH_TRAINING`; start and end of the workout; Android title is the workout name and zone offsets come from `originalOffsetMinutes`; iOS metadata `workoutBrandName: 'BeyondRep'`, `indoorWorkout: true`. **No heart rate, calories, distance, sets or reps**: the phone has none of its own and nothing is invented. Watch-origin (`beyondrep_watch`) workouts and outside workouts are never written.
3. **Identity and versions.** The platform id is the lowercase workout UUID (iOS `syncIdentifier`, Android `clientRecordId`) and the version is the workout's `updatedAtMs` (iOS `syncVersion`, Android `clientRecordVersion`). `updatedAtMs` only grows, unlike the local `revision` that restarts when a workout is restored on another phone, so a rewrite from any phone carries a greater version and replaces the earlier entry instead of adding a copy. No native code: the `health` 14.0.0 plugin already exposes this.
4. **Receipts.** `HealthExportReceiptEntity` (local ObjectBox only) per workout: state, exported version, platform record id, attempts, last attempt. States: `writing`, `confirmed`, `failedRetryable`, `blocked`, `skippedWatch`, `supersededByWatch`, `deleting`. A receipt is committed as `writing` before the platform call and as `confirmed` after it. `HealthExportSettingEntity` holds the switch. Neither entity is synced, exported or logged.
5. **Derive, do not enqueue.** An exporter pass compares completed phone workouts with receipts and does the missing writes and deletes. The finish and delete transactions and the cloud outbox are untouched. Backfill, retry and new workouts share one path. Triggers: finish, delete, watch import or `workout_link`, switch on, app resume, sign-in. One pass at a time, at most 50 writes per pass, oldest first. Retry backoff for `failedRetryable`: 2^(attempts-1) minutes, capped at 60.
6. **Crash safety.** On iOS, before the first write of a workout, the exporter reads BeyondRep's own saved workouts in a one-minute window around it and adopts an exact match (same start and end to the second) instead of writing again. Android upserts by client record id and needs no adopt step.
7. **Never duplicate the watch (iOS).** A workout with a watch receipt that carries an HKWorkout UUID is never written. Before the first write, a workout from a child source of `in.beyondrep.app` (the watch app) that overlaps by at least half of the shorter makes the receipt `skippedWatch`. A `workout_link` or watch import that arrives after the phone saved deletes our copy and marks the receipt `supersededByWatch`. With a paired Apple Watch that has the app installed, a workout is not written until 10 minutes after it finished.
8. **Delete follows.** Deleting a workout in BeyondRep deletes our copy (iOS `deleteByUUID`, Android `deleteByClientRecordId`) and removes the receipt. A deleted workout with no receipt (deleted on another phone) gets the same own-copy lookup on iOS. A denied delete keeps the receipt `blocked` and is retried. With the switch off nothing is written or deleted; receipts are kept and acted on if it is turned back on.
9. **Permissions.** iOS `HealthDataType.WORKOUT` with `READ_WRITE` (the read side is needed for the own-copy and watch checks); Android `android.permission.health.WRITE_EXERCISE` beside the existing `READ_EXERCISE`. `NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription` becomes "BeyondRep writes the workouts you finish on this phone to Apple Health when you turn on saving in the Health tab." Nothing else.
10. **Account boundary.** Settings and receipts live in the account's ObjectBox partition. A pass started for an account stops before its next platform call when the signed-in account changes. Account deletion keeps entries already in the health store (they are the user's health records now); local receipts go with the partition.

## Accepted risk

HealthKit never reveals whether read access was granted: a denied Workout read returns nothing, so the watch-overlap and own-copy checks find nothing. The exporter still writes in that case. Same-phone cases stay covered by the watch receipt, the 10 minute delay and the superseded delete. The remaining exposure is a phone restored from the cloud with Workout read denied, for workouts the watch joined: one extra entry in Apple Health is possible there. The user can delete it in the Health app.

A deleted workout with no receipt is looked up in HealthKit on each iOS pass (a small read per such workout). If that proves slow on a long history, a "looked up" marker can be added without a policy change.

## Consequences

- Platform ids, receipts and the switch stay in ObjectBox. Nothing about the export reaches Supabase, the outbox, the JSON export, logs or telemetry. `package:health` is imported only by `platform_health_reader.dart` and `health_workout_writer.dart` (the import guard test lists both); the exporter reads ObjectBox, so it lives in `features/health/data/`, not `application/`.
- Our own saved workouts are dropped from the outside-workout read (`in.beyondrep.app` source), so they never count as outside workouts or feed training load twice.
- The Wear OS stage extends `HealthWorkoutRecord` with optional heart-rate samples and calories later; this ADR does not add them.
- The App Store review notes, the App Privacy answers, the Play Health apps declaration and the privacy policy inputs mention the write permission before release ([`PRIVACY_POLICY_INPUTS.md`](../PRIVACY_POLICY_INPUTS.md)).
- Changing what is written (energy, heart rate, sets), writing watch or outside workouts, or background export needs a new ADR.
- Status: implemented in source; device rows are open in [`docs/RELEASE.md`](../RELEASE.md).
