# ADR-0011 — Read workouts from health platforms (Phase 3 extension)

**Status:** **Accepted** — decided by the human owner on 2026-11-26 (design approved). Implemented in source, **not released or device-verified**.
**Amends:** [ADR-0009](0009-health-insights-trends.md) (its "training-load readiness input not implemented" line, and the read-type list in item 1) and [ADR-0008](0008-daily-health-summary-reads.md) item 1. Does not alter ADR-0005 backup scope or ADR-0003 ownership. Design: [`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-health-polish-and-outside-workouts-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-health-polish-and-outside-workouts-design.md) (Stage 2).

## Context

People log some training outside BeyondRep: a run, a ride, a class, or the same lift on an Apple Watch. Those workouts are in HealthKit and Health Connect. Showing them next to BeyondRep sessions, merging the duplicates and counting them toward recent training load gives a fuller history and a better readiness score. They are physiological platform data, so the "read fresh, store nothing" rule of ADR-0009 must hold, and a new read type needs a numbered ADR.

## Decision

1. **Workouts from other apps are read fresh, joined in memory and never stored.** `PlatformHealthReader.readWorkouts` returns `OutsideWorkout` values (platform id, friendly activity type, start, end, active kcal and distance when present, source name). The health overview reads the last 90 local days with its other reads and holds them in memory only. Nothing is written to ObjectBox, Supabase, the outbox, the export file, logs or telemetry, and nothing is backed up. Average heart rate for a workout comes from `HEART_RATE` samples inside its window, is read on demand, is nullable and is never invented. The only thing that changes on disk is the existing non-health `currentHealthAccessScope` integer (now 3), so already-connected accounts see "Allow more data" once.

2. **Read scope.** `HealthDataType.WORKOUT` is added to both request lists. Android declares `READ_EXERCISE` and **nothing else for workouts**: no total-calories or distance permission. Android workouts therefore show no kcal and no distance (the plugin returns null without those permissions). iOS kcal is the workout's active energy and distance is its walking/running/cycling distance when HealthKit has it. `NSHealthShareUsageDescription` now says "workouts" and "training history". Health Connect may return less than 90 days (30 days before the grant without the history permission); that is accepted.

3. **Our own writes are dropped.** Any workout whose `sourceId` or `sourceName` is `in.beyondrep.app` or an `in.beyondrep.app.` child (the iPhone app and its watch app `in.beyondrep.app.watchkitapp`) is ignored, and so is any workout whose platform UUID is in a stored watch workout receipt (ADR-0012). *Amended 2026-11-27 for Phase 4 stage 4.*

4. **Merge rule.** Outside workouts that several sources wrote for the same activity (for example Samsung Health and Fit, or Strava copying an Apple Watch run) are first collapsed to one: two overlapping by at least 50% of the shorter are the same workout, and the one with kcal is kept, then the longer, then the earlier start. Then an outside workout merges into a completed BeyondRep session when their time ranges overlap by at least 50% of the shorter of the two durations. Each outside workout merges into at most one session (the largest overlap; on a tie the earlier session); one session can absorb several. Calories add up; average heart rate is computed from samples inside the BeyondRep session's own window. Only completed sessions with a completion time participate. The merge runs in an application-layer provider over the overview and the read-only completed-workout history (as the stage 3 training insights do), so a session is never counted twice because a watch recorded the same lift.

5. **Where they show.** Activity detail (a Workouts panel with minutes per day and a list), Progress history (rows tagged "From <source>" among BeyondRep sessions inside the selected period, a read-only detail page, "From other apps" under the empty state), and the Today Workouts tile (BeyondRep plus workouts that did not merge). When workouts are not allowed or health is disconnected, Progress and Today look exactly as before. Outside workouts do **not** change muscle recovery, the Progress "N finished" count, volume or strength charts, the Today "This week" dots, "Better fit today", sync, backup or export.

6. **Training load is a readiness input (amends ADR-0009).** Session load is minutes times intensity. BeyondRep: minutes are completed minus started; intensity is the mean RPE of completed working sets that have an RPE divided by 10, or 0.8 when none are rated. Outside: minutes are end minus start; intensity is 0.7 (there is no reliable maximum heart rate without an age). Merged outside workouts are not counted separately. The ratio is r = (7-day load / 7) / (28-day load / 28) over the days before today, today excluded; it needs at least 14 days between the earliest session in the 28-day window and today and a non-zero 28-day load, otherwise the input is absent, never zero. Part score: r up to 0.8 gives 90; 0.8 to 1.3 falls linearly from 90 to 70; 1.3 to 1.8 from 70 to 30; 1.8 and above gives 30. The base weight is 0.15 beside HRV 0.40, resting HR 0.25, sleep 0.25 and sleep debt 0.10, rescaled over the parts present. A history read failure means no load part at all (nothing can be merged, so counting outside workouts could count a session twice); readiness still scores from the other inputs. The 7/30-day readiness trend scores load for past days the same way from the same read, so today's trend point equals the hero. These weights and bands are a starting point to tune after device use.

7. **Copy and privacy.** "Training load" and "Workouts from other apps" get explanation sheets and glossary entries. The privacy policy inputs (sections 2.6 and 7.2) and the website privacy page list exercise sessions / workouts among the read types, with `READ_EXERCISE` on Android, read fresh and never stored. If calories or distance permissions are ever added they must be listed there first.

   | Approach | Trade-off | Outcome |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Read fresh, merge in memory, store nothing | Keeps ADR-0009's promise, no purge logic; at most 90 days, merge recomputed on each overview read | **Chosen** |
   | Store outside workouts or load per day in ObjectBox | Longer history, faster; needs purge on revoke/disconnect, a policy change and a new ADR | Rejected for now |
   | Add `READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED` and `READ_DISTANCE` on Android | Kcal and distance for Android workouts; two more permissions and declarations | Rejected by the owner for now |

## Consequences

- No outside workout, its kcal, heart rate, distance or the training load may reach Supabase, the outbox, the export, ObjectBox, logs or telemetry. Existing guards stay (`package:health` only in `platform_health_reader.dart`; `features/sync`, `features/account` and `core/storage` never import `features/health`). `features/health/domain` may additionally import the pure workout value types in `features/workouts/domain/workout_models.dart` for the merge and the load.
- Readiness scores differ slightly from stage 1 once 14 days of workout history exist. With fewer days or no load in 28 days they are unchanged.
- The Play Console Health apps declaration gains `READ_EXERCISE`, and the App Store review notes mention reading workouts.
- Adding storage of these values, writing workouts back, or another permission needs a new ADR.
- Status: implemented in source; the device matrix (iPhone with Watch logging the same lift, a third-party app workout, Android 14+ with and without the history permission, permission added after connect, account switch) is open in [`docs/RELEASE.md`](../RELEASE.md).
